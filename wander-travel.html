Who we are

Slow travel, planned down to the small things

Wander started in 2019 with one trip and eight friends of friends. The rule has not changed since: we plan a route we would take ourselves, then travel it with you.

That means smaller vans, longer stops and evenings without a schedule. We book the beds, arrange the transfers and keep a host with the group the whole time — so you spend the trip looking around instead of working out the timetable.

Why small

Eight people fit around one table, into two cars and through a village without changing it.

That is the whole idea. Above eight, the group becomes a crowd: the host stops knowing everyone, restaurants need booking and the day has to run to a schedule.

FAQ

Questions before you book

How fit do I need to be?

For the coast trip you should be comfortable walking four to five hours with a day pack — not fast, just steady. The desert and city trips involve almost no walking. Every route lists the hardest day in the plan, so you can judge it honestly.

What is included in the price?

All accommodation, transport once the trip starts, the host and any entry tickets listed in the plan. Flights, most meals and personal spending are not included. We keep meals separate on purpose: it is cheaper, and you decide where you eat.

I am travelling on my own — is that awkward?

Roughly half of every group books alone. You will share a twin room with another traveller of the same gender unless you pay the single supplement. Nobody has ever asked to switch rooms mid-trip.

What if I have to cancel?

More than four weeks before departure, we move your deposit to any other date in the next twelve months. After that the deposit is lost, because the beds are already paid for. There is no insurance tied to the booking — take out your own.

Can you arrange a private departure?

Yes, if you are six people or more. The route stays the same, the dates become yours, and the price per person usually drops a little. Write to us with the month you have in mind.
